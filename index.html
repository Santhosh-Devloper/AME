Asian Mag Exim Inc is a reputed manufacturer, exporter, and import supplier headquartered in India, specializing in premium-grade refractory minerals, magnesium sulphate products, and steel plant consumables.

We combine over two decades of industrial expertise with rigorous quality standards to deliver products that meet global specifications. Our supply chain serves steel plants, foundries, and mineral processing units across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and beyond.
